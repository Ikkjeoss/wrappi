About Wrappi

We believe great packaging
builds great brands

Wrappi was founded in 2019 with a single mission: make professional packaging and reliable delivery accessible to every business, no matter the size.

Today we serve over 5,000 businesses across 120+ cities, processing millions of packages every year. Our team of packaging experts, logistics engineers, and designers works tirelessly to help our clients turn every delivery into a memorable brand experience.

  • ISO-certified quality assurance on every order
  • Dedicated account manager for Business & Enterprise plans
  • Carbon-neutral shipping available on all routes
  • 24/7 customer support via chat, email, and phone
